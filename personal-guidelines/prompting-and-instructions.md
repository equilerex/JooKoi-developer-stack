# Prompting and instructions — personal takes

Personal, not consensus. Accumulated opinions on writing instructions for AI agents, meant to actively guide picks elsewhere in this repo. Expected to keep growing — not a one-time write-up. Migrated from the (now-archived, see `_architecture/archive/2026-08.md`) `TODO-LIST.md` §5b draft notes, 2026-08-27; wording still rough in places, refine as it grows.

- Build from your own repeated need, not from marketplace browsing.
- context is king but Less is more — every instruction the model receives is another chance for misinterpretation.
- It's more important to get your ideas across than to hand over specific rules/regulations.
- A long, normal explanation — talk, rant, prose, personal manifesto — it's better to over-explain than write a word or two and assume the model understands you. (for one off replies, where the llm can clean it up and organize for further use - also helps to confirm it understands your intent)
- Think about context as much as possible — when telling the model to do something, explain *why*, give it a concept or a bar to measure the task against, not just the instruction itself. (Phrasing still rough, wants to refine wording later.)
