## 2026-09-02 — jookoi-doc redesign: TODO.md replaces current-state.md + next-steps.md, progress.md retired

Full redesign of the paper-trail pipeline, requested directly by the user after diagnosing the old pipeline's friction: a per-session-feeling forced flush, a continuously-resequenced `next-steps.md`, and a `progress.md` middle stage that added a stage without adding value.

Design calls made during the build: `doc-gate.sh` stops demanding a flush — its job is now nudging a `TODO.md` update when the tree is dirty and the file hasn't been touched, never a flush. `flush` takes the whole of `TODO.md` verbatim as the archive entry body and does not parse checkbox state — moving anything still relevant to `BACKLOG.md` is the model's job, done before calling flush.
